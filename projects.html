CarLog — Vehicle Maintenance & Cost Tracking App

2-Person Course Project · 2025

A full-stack app for tracking vehicle maintenance, fuel, mileage, and repair costs. I was the primary developer: I owned the data model, the REST API design, and the end-to-end integration between the Flutter client and the Flask backend.
